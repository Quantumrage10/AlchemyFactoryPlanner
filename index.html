How the bus line is drawn, and what the four labels mean
A module makes everything it needs inside its own tile, from coins, whenever that fits. What you see below is only our recommended starting layout. Every item has a picker, so you can put anything anywhere:
  • Yes · own wagons It gets its own module and its own tagged wagons. Recommended when a module that needs it can't fit making it.
  • Yes · shared wagon It rides a wagon type with other items. This works when the place it's going takes any of them: a furnace takes any fuel, a nursery any fertilizer, research any relic. You can still unload one particular item off a shared wagon by filtering on the cargo.
  • Maybe Packed-down items that only one or two things use. They fit inside the module that uses them, or you can bus them. Your call.
  • No Made right where it's used, or next to the shop.
Shared wagons push. If you make too much of one item, it can fill the shared wagons before another item gets picked up. If that bites, give the item its own wagons.
Shared wagons. Items in the same box ride one wagon type between them. Rename or delete a wagon, make your own, and add items here or with the picker in the table below.
