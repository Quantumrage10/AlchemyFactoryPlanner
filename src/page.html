How the bus line is drawn. A module makes everything it needs inside its own tile, from coins, whenever that fits. Each item then lands in one of four places:
  • Yes · own wagons A module that needs it can't fit making it, so it gets its own module and its own tagged wagons. Coke powder and advanced fertilizer are always here.
  • Yes · shared wagon Relics. They only go to the shop and to research, so they can all ride one "research" wagon type instead of one each.
  • Maybe Packed-down items that only one or two things use. They fit inside the module that uses them, or you can give them their own module and bus them. Your call.
  • No Bulk, raw, grown or liquid stuff, and things that are only sold. Made right where they're used, or next to the shop.
Press "Put it on the bus" or "Keep it off the bus" on any item and every module that uses it updates to match.
