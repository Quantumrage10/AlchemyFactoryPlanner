How the bus line is drawn
A module makes everything it needs inside its own tile, from coins, when that fits. The layout below is a recommendation; change any item with its picker.
  • Yes · own wagons Its own module and its own tagged wagons.
  • Yes · shared wagon Rides one wagon type with other items. Works where the destination takes any of them: furnaces, nurseries, the shop and research. You can still unload one item by filtering on cargo.
  • Maybe Fits inside the module that uses it, or can be bussed. Your call.
  • No Made where it's used, or next to the shop.
Shared wagons push. Too much of one item can fill the wagons before another gets picked up. If that happens, give the item its own wagons.
